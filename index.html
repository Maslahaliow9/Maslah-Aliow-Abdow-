About Me

A little about who I am and what I do.

Maslah Aliow Abdow is an 18-year-old Kenyan researcher, founder, freelancer, innovator, and technology enthusiast with interests spanning Artificial Intelligence, education, software, entrepreneurship, scientific discovery, and emerging technologies.


He enjoys turning ideas into practical solutions and working on projects that address real-world needs, gaining experience in digital product development, web technologies, AI-powered applications, research, and independent project creation.


He is also a freelancer, taking on digital and technology-related work while continuing to expand his skills and experience. His approach: research, create, test, solve problems, and improve.

«You don't need to know everything to build something meaningful. You need curiosity, initiative, persistence, and the willingness to keep learning.»

Education

Maslah studied at Takaba Boys' Senior School, a well-regarded secondary school in Kenya.


His academic background has helped shape his interests in science, research, communication, technology, and problem-solving.
