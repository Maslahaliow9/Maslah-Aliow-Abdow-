About Me

A little about who I am and what I do.

Maslah Aliow is a Kenyan student, aspiring developer and young journalist with an interest in technology, education, communication and digital innovation.


Alongside his education, Maslah explores software development and digital technologies, with a particular interest in creating practical tools that can solve everyday problems.


He is also interested in journalism and enjoys communicating information, discussing issues and creating useful content for his community.


His long-term goal is to continue developing his skills in technology, education, communication and entrepreneurship while creating projects that can positively impact other people.
